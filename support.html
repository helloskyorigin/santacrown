Payments and ranking

If you were charged but your name or URL does not appear, check your checkout receipt and contact support with your transaction reference, the public URL and the approximate time of payment. A return-page message alone does not confirm a rank; only a verified payment can update the leaderboard.

Repeat payments made to the same normalized public URL count toward that entry's verified cumulative total. Additional buyers can change the standings at any time.

Refunds and mistaken charges

Our general policy is no refunds for a correctly completed digital leaderboard purchase. We do review duplicate, unauthorized, incorrect and undelivered charges, along with any refunds required by applicable law or payment-provider terms. Please read our No-Refund Policy.

Report an inappropriate link

Send the public URL and a short explanation to our support inbox. We review reports about impersonation, malware, fraud, unlawful content and privacy concerns. Please do not include private passwords or complete payment-card details.

No account required

You do not need a SantaCrown account to browse the public board or use the free preview. Once enabled, secure checkout may ask for billing details through the payment provider. A public URL is not an ownership credential for editing a listing.
