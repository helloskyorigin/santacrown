Rules for using the Christmas leaderboard

Current launch status

SantaCrown currently offers a free preview. No real leaderboard position is sold or reserved until checkout is explicitly enabled following merchant approval, database integration and final commercial disclosure. This preview does not collect payments.

How the paid ranking would work

One canonical public website or social URL identifies one listing. The first verified qualifying payment sets its public display name. Verified successful contributions to that URL are added in USD cents, minus refunded or disputed credits. Highest net verified total ranks first; ties are decided by the first verified listing timestamp, then canonical URL. A repeat visitor chooses a new target total and pays the difference calculated by the server at checkout. Two people could pay for the same URL independently; their actual verified payments both count, which can take the total above the earlier target.

Amount, fees and taxes

The website may offer suggested amounts, but the customer enters their own target. Payment-processor transaction minimums, maximums, currency handling, taxes and other limitations apply. Rank credit reflects the eligible product price rather than additional tax or fees. The final payable amount is shown in the payment provider checkout; if the quote differs from what the visitor expects, they should cancel and contact support.

Christmas honors and event cutoff

The top three positions are intended to receive disclosed Christmas-themed display honors; no cash prize, lottery or randomly selected winner is offered. Exact benefits, duration, ranking cutoff (including timezone), appeal procedure and service availability must be published before real payments are enabled. No position is promised before the cutoff, and live standings may change as other verified payments arrive or a payment is refunded.

Public names, URLs and moderation

Only submit a name and a public link you have permission to publish. Do not impersonate another person, link to illegal or malicious content, promote fraud, or post abusive or infringing names. A submitted URL does not establish ownership. SantaCrown may hide prohibited listings and investigate reports without deleting underlying financial records. Ownership verification will be required before changing a listing or claiming personalized honors.

Billing problems and disputes

A payment is counted only after a confirmed processor webhook. Payment redirects alone cannot create rank. Refunds and chargebacks can reduce or suspend rank credit. Report suspected duplicate charges or payment discrepancies to the support contact below with a transaction reference. Do not send full card details.

Availability and limitations

We may suspend abusive activity and take the site offline for maintenance, security incidents or provider restrictions. Participation does not guarantee profile views, sales, promotional exposure beyond explicitly described honors, or any financial return. Visitors follow third-party links at their own risk. Nothing here removes statutory consumer rights.
