What SantaCrown provides

SantaCrown is a seasonal, public Christmas-themed ranking experience. After live checkout is approved and enabled, participants will be able to pay for a displayed public entry identified by a website or social-profile URL. Payment is for the defined digital leaderboard/display service, not a donation, investment, wager or promise of promotion results.

There is no required SantaCrown account. Checkout may require details requested by the independent payment provider. We currently offer only a free preview.

Ranking, names and links

One canonical public URL represents one listing. The first verified qualifying payment sets the public display name. Additional verified payments toward the same URL increase its cumulative total, even if made by someone else. The person entering a public URL does not automatically prove ownership or gain permission to edit its name.

Ranking uses net verified USD purchase amounts, highest first. Refunded or disputed payment credits are reduced or excluded. Ties are resolved by the earliest confirmed entry time and then the canonical URL. Other participants can outbid your entry at any time; paying does not secure a rank for a fixed period.

Only the payment-provider confirmation and our verified transaction ledger establish paid credit. Screenshots, redirects and pending checkouts do not.

Payments and target amounts

You enter your Christmas name, public URL and desired cumulative total. If an entry already exists for that same URL, the server calculates the amount needed to reach the new target based on the current verified total. The total may change before checkout if another payment arrives.

Before paying, review the exact amount, applicable taxes and payment currency displayed at secure checkout. Payment-provider limits and local rules may apply. Purchases are intended to be one-time, not recurring subscriptions. No entry is earned until payment is verified.

Final sales and refunds

Completed paid leaderboard purchases are generally final and non-refundable. We do not offer discretionary refunds because you changed your mind, another participant outranked you, you wanted a different position, or your external link received fewer visitors than expected.

This rule does not override refunds or remedies required by law, applicable checkout-provider terms, or valid cases involving unauthorized/duplicate charges, an incorrect charge or a purchased service that was not delivered as promised. See the No-Refund Policy before making a purchase.

Christmas Top-3 honors and cutoff

SantaCrown may recognize the final Top 3 with disclosed seasonal display features (for example a Christmas homepage message). There are no cash prizes, random draws or guaranteed earnings. Final reward details, placement durations, precise cutoff date and time zone, claim/verification process, and eligibility restrictions will be published before live payments begin.

Until those details are published, preview positions are not paid reservations and no Christmas honor is promised. An outbid rank or removed prohibited entry does not, by itself, entitle a buyer to a refund.

Acceptable names and external links

Only submit public names and URLs you are allowed to share. Do not impersonate people or brands, use harmful or unlawful links, spread malware or spam, infringe intellectual property, or upload private information. Outbound links take visitors to third-party websites that SantaCrown does not control or endorse.

We may hide, restrict or remove abusive entries and prevent new purchases for them. Hiding a listing does not remove financial transaction records. A verified owner may need to prove control before changing a listing or claiming personalized honors. Report inappropriate entries to hello.skyorigin@gmail.com.

Availability, responsibility and age

Leaderboard placement does not guarantee views, clicks, sales, reach, or a permanent rank. Technical maintenance, platform moderation, provider restrictions and legal requirements may affect availability. You must be legally eligible to make an online payment in your jurisdiction and use the payment method with authorization.

Nothing in these terms takes away non-waivable consumer rights. If a payment provider is displayed as Merchant of Record at checkout, its applicable buyer terms also govern payment processing, taxes and billing disputes.
