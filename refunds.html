Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective: 14 November 2026 · Pre-launch

SantaCrown has not enabled real payments. Published commercial terms must be updated and explicitly approved before a paid launch.

Clear information before checkout

Pre-launch status

SantaCrown is currently a free preview. Real checkout is disabled, so no SantaCrown purchase, paid position or refund can occur through this site yet.

Once purchases are available

The merchant or reseller shown during checkout will provide the applicable billing terms. Before payments are enabled, this page will contain final, approved refund and cancellation criteria, delivery timing and any mandatory consumer protections. We will not silently treat checkout as enabled while this page remains a pre-launch policy.

Payment not confirmed

Submitting a form, opening checkout, seeing a payment redirect or viewing a screenshot does not prove a successful charge. A paid leaderboard entry is created only after a verified provider payment confirmation. If you were charged but no entry appears, contact support with the receipt/reference, date and public URL.

Refunds and ranking

Where a payment is refunded, fully or partially, qualifying rank credit is reduced in proportion to that refund. A disputed payment may be excluded while the dispute is open and adjusted according to its final resolution. These measures avoid showing unpaid ranking totals. This is not a promise that every purchase is refundable; applicable law and the displayed checkout terms govern.

Requesting support

Email hello.skyorigin@gmail.com with the approximate transaction date, payment reference, amount, public link and a brief description of the issue. Do not share complete credit-card data. We will direct requests to the relevant payment provider/merchant when necessary.

Statutory rights

Nothing on this page limits consumer rights provided by applicable law. We will not process live purchases before the approved commercial refund terms are published and available to customers.
