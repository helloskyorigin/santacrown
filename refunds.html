THE SHORT VERSION

Completed purchases are generally non-refundable. We do not refund because a rank changes, a buyer changes their mind, or traffic is lower than expected. Billing errors, non-delivery, provider rules and statutory rights are exceptions.

Our general no-refund rule

Once a qualifying payment is confirmed and the purchased leaderboard display is delivered, the sale is final. We generally do not provide voluntary refunds or cancellations for a change of mind or a correctly delivered digital placement.

Not normally refundable:
  • You change your mind after paying.
  • Another buyer passes your rank or your listing drops from the Top 3.
  • You submit an additional payment toward the same public URL.
  • You expected more clicks, followers, traffic or sales than you received.

Situations we will review

“No refunds” does not mean we can keep money for a failed or unauthorized transaction. We will review and provide the refund or other remedy required under applicable law and binding payment-provider terms, including where appropriate:

  • A payment was duplicated, unauthorized or charged for the wrong amount.
  • The purchased digital placement was not delivered as promised after a confirmed charge.
  • A valid statutory consumer right or provider/bank decision requires a refund.

Do not assume every report automatically qualifies; we review payment and delivery evidence. Dodo Payments, if used as Merchant of Record, may decide or process refunds under its own buyer terms.

Before and after checkout

Before confirming payment, you can leave checkout without purchasing. Completing a form or seeing a preview does not place an order. After a successful purchase and delivery, the normal final-sale rule applies. There are no recurring SantaCrown subscription charges planned.

If a payment is still pending or fails, it does not add rank credit. If you see a charge but no verified entry appears, contact support instead of paying again immediately.

Effect of a refund or chargeback

When an amount is refunded, its corresponding eligible leaderboard credit is reversed or reduced. An open dispute can temporarily exclude that payment from the ranking; the final outcome determines whether the credit remains. This keeps positions based on verified net purchases rather than unpaid balances.

How to report a billing problem

Email hello.skyorigin@gmail.com with the subject SantaCrown Billing Support. Include your payment/receipt reference, approximate purchase date, charged amount, public URL, and a short description. Do not send your full card number, CVV, password or one-time code.

We will review the report and coordinate with the applicable processor when needed. If legally applicable, you also retain the right to contact the payment provider or your bank.

Current payment status

At the time of this policy update, SantaCrown is a pre-launch free preview. Live checkout is disabled and we are not currently selling leaderboard positions. Before paid checkout opens, the final merchant details and any mandatory jurisdiction-specific notices will be published.
