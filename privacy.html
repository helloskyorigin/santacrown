Information we collect

During the free preview, you can enter a Christmas display name, public website or social link and a target amount. Those values are used for on-screen previews, and are not stored as a paid public entry simply by typing into the form. Our hosting provider may process basic technical request details such as IP address, device/browser type, timestamps, pages requested and security logs.

When payments are enabled, SantaCrown is designed to store a canonical public URL, display name, eligible payment and refund references, amounts in USD cents, creation times, payment state, and fraud-prevention signals. The checkout provider independently collects any billing/contact information it requires.

What appears publicly

For verified paid entries that are not hidden for moderation, the Christmas name, public link, net eligible total and current rank appear on SantaCrown. Public information can be viewed and captured by other people. Avoid submitting phone numbers, private account information, or sensitive personal details as names or URLs.

A public URL alone is not proof of ownership. Changes to a listing or claiming personalized honors may require verification.

How we use information

We use data to operate and sort the leaderboard, confirm purchases and refunds, prevent fraud/abuse, respond to reports and support requests, fulfill legal accounting requirements and protect the service. Depending on jurisdiction, lawful grounds may include delivering a requested service, legal obligations, legitimate interests, or consent where required.

Providers and international processing

Vercel hosts the site. Supabase is intended to hold the transaction ledger when configured. Dodo Payments is the planned checkout provider and, where applicable, Merchant of Record. Google Fonts may receive connection data when the font is fetched. These services may process information in countries other than yours and under their own policies.

SantaCrown does not sell personal information to advertisers or intentionally operate advertising-retargeting pixels in the current site. We do not store raw payment card numbers or CVV data.

Retention and safeguarding

Preview form values are not intended to be saved in a customer account by SantaCrown. When paid services launch, payment, fraud, legal and support records may need to be kept for applicable accounting, security, dispute-resolution and legal requirements, even after a public entry is hidden. We use transport security and server-side verification, although no system can be guaranteed risk-free.

Specific retention requirements depend on the final merchant/operator arrangement and the laws that apply. These will be reviewed before live launch.

Your privacy choices and rights

Contact hello.skyorigin@gmail.com to ask about your information, request correction or removal of public details, or exercise other rights available under applicable law. We may request reasonable identity or link-control verification before making changes. Financial transaction records cannot always be deleted immediately where retention is legally required.

Age and policy changes

SantaCrown is not designed for children to make payments. People making purchases must be legally eligible and authorized to use the payment method. We will update this page when the live operator, processor configuration, retention details or data practices change. A date will accompany material changes.
