Privacy Policy

Effective: November 14, 2026

SantaCrown is currently a preview. No real payments, paid positions or purchases are enabled.

Information you enter

On the current preview, the Christmas name, public profile link and proposed amount are handled in your browser. They are used to create an on-screen preview and optional downloadable greeting card; they are not submitted to SantaCrown's servers by the preview form. No public entry is created or stored. Refreshing the page clears the form values unless your browser chooses to remember them.

Hosting and technical information

As with most hosted websites, Vercel and underlying infrastructure may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser, request path and diagnostic logs, to deliver the site and protect its operation. External Google Fonts are requested for typography and may receive standard connection data. We do not currently use advertising pixels or intentionally set marketing tracking cookies on this preview.

Public links and third parties

If you click an external website or social profile, you leave SantaCrown. That third party may process information under its own policy. The contact email link opens your chosen email application; messages you send are used to respond to your request.

Future payments and leaderboard records

Payment collection is not currently enabled. Before any live payments, we will update this notice to describe the personal information and purchase records collected, their purposes, retention periods, payment processors, and any public profile information. Confirmed public rankings, if launched, would show only the information described at that time.

Policy changes
