Privacy Policy

Effective: 14 November 2026 · Pre-launch

SantaCrown has not enabled real payments. Published commercial terms must be updated and explicitly approved before a paid launch.

How we handle details you share

What we collect in this preview

Name, public URL and target amount stay in the browser during the free preview; no public listing or order is created. A downloaded greeting image is generated locally in the browser. Hosting infrastructure may process IP addresses, device/browser information, requested paths, timestamps, abuse signals and diagnostic logs.

Information once payments are enabled

The backend design stores a canonical public link, display name, verified payment and refund transaction references, amounts in USD cents, timing, payment status, and nonreversible abuse-prevention signals. Dodo and other payment providers, once configured, separately process payment, billing and compliance details according to their terms. SantaCrown does not need or store raw card numbers. Specific processors, retention periods and the merchant identity will be confirmed before live checkout.

Public display

A successful and non-hidden public entry will show the display name, canonical link and net verified total/rank. Do not submit private or sensitive data in a name or public URL. Public pages can be viewed, indexed or captured by others. A public link does not grant the submitter permission to change an existing listing.

Purpose and legal basis

We use information to provide the leaderboard, reconcile purchases and reversals, prevent fraud and abuse, meet legal accounting obligations, respond to support requests and protect service security. Where applicable, processing relies on contract performance, legal obligations, legitimate interests or the consent/rights required under relevant data-protection law.

Hosting, providers and transfers

The site runs on Vercel. Google Fonts may receive basic connection information if requested by the browser. When configured, Supabase provides database hosting and Dodo handles checkout and payment workflows. Providers may process data in other countries. We do not intentionally add ad retargeting pixels or sell visitor information to advertisers.

Retention and safety

Preview form values are not durably stored by our application. Live ledger rows, transaction identifiers and support records may need retention to resolve disputes and comply with accounting and legal requirements. We will publish applicable durations and deletion limitations before a paid launch; deleting a visible listing is different from deleting legally required payment records.

Your rights

You can contact us to ask about access, correction, removal of public listing data, or other applicable privacy rights. Verification may be needed before changing a listing attributed to a public URL. Some transaction records must be retained under law. Do not email passwords or payment-card numbers.

Security and updates

We use HTTPS, server-side payment verification, access controls and limits intended to protect the service, but no online system is risk-free. Material changes to this policy will be dated and published. Contact us about a suspected security issue.
